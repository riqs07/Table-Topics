Venues, Sage, and a blob with more to say

Three mockups in direction B. 1. A course path where each lesson is a bigger room and the audience grows. 2. Sage, the green coach, as the app’s voice on four screens, with the orange blob as you. 3. A lab where you can trigger the orange blob’s new “moments” and see the pose sheet.

Phones

1 · The venue path

“Stop freezing” as a journey from the mirror to a live radio studio. Every lesson stays open; the rooms show where you’re heading, not a lock.

The rooms

Why it works

  • Public speaking really is about a growing audience, so the map means something.
  • The boss isn’t a random end node: Benny is waiting in the last room.
  • Each course gets its own set of rooms (a wedding for Toasts, a lift for Explain it simply), so no two paths look alike.
  • Rooms are scenes, not icons: the same drawing style as the course tiles.

2 · Sage, the coach, and where Sage goes

Sage speaks for the app; the orange blob is you. Sage appears at the four moments a coach would: greeting you, steadying you before you speak, teaching, and giving one note after.

3 · The orange blob, more expressive

Still most of the time: a breath and a blink. Then one clear move when something happens, with a wind-up, an overshoot and a settle. Tap a moment.

Idle: a slow breath and a blink every few seconds. Nothing else.

Pose sheet: eight still poses

Body language does more than motion. These are drawn once and reused everywhere: Today, results, scenes, videos.
