VoiceLab Chart Kit

One chart language for VoiceLab

A redesign sketch: every chart is built from the same five marks, answers one question in its headline, compares you with yourself, and never uses red. Hover any mark for its value. Example data, not a real user.

The kit Your voice, as a ribbon A gap is a real pause A dot is a pause word Pitch, one soft line Your last time, as a ghost

The rules behind these

  • Headline first: the chart only proves the sentence above it.
  • Your words are the axis wherever possible; time only when words won’t fit.
  • Always compared with you: last time or day 1 as a dashed ghost.
  • No red. Teal for what went well, apricot for pause words, never “bad”.
  • One axis, thin marks, rounded ends, light grid; a value on hover, not on every mark.

Colour, checked

  • Voice (indigo), pause word (apricot) and went-well (teal) pass the colour-blind separation checks in light and dark.
  • They also differ by shape (ribbon, dot, band), so nothing relies on colour alone.
  • Dark mode uses its own steps, validated against the dark card.
  • Charts draw themselves once, then stay still (off with reduced motion).
