# Design System

**Purpose:** How you control colors, fonts, and feel, and the rules that keep the look in one place.
**Last updated:** 2026-10-22 (decisions 114 to 118)
**Status:** Built in milestone 2. **Calm studio with a forest green accent was chosen at C6 (2026-10-12).** The other two themes stay in `src/theme/themes.ts`; the style and wireframe pages that showed them side by side were removed in decision 105.

## Warm and lively (decision 69, 2026-10-19)

The user found the script page stiff and asked for something more fun. These rules sit on top of the script page below and win wherever the two disagree (the older rules are kept for their history).

- **Type.** Headlines, page titles, section headings, card names, the action band and every big number are in **Fraunces** (semibold, slightly tight); running text is **Atkinson Hyperlegible**. Both are set by the calm studio theme (`src/theme/fonts.ts`, `themes.ts`).
- **New colour roles**: `card` (white panels lifted off the page), `warm` (the apricot accent: today's square, the next step's number, the done badge, the live dot), `warm-tint` (its soft background: the next step's row, the mission card, icon tiles, the "One change" pill), `warm-ink` (text on the tint) and `on-warm` (text and ticks on `warm`). The contrast test covers every pair they are used in.
- **Areas** (`data-area`). Three parts of the app get their own surfaces, defined per theme in `themes.areas` and written by `themeCss.ts`; while one is showing, the whole page takes its colours so the tab bar and edges match:
  - `stage`: deep green with light ink while the clock runs (thinking and recording).
  - `results`: a soft green on the session page.
  - `learn`: sand on Learn, a set's page and a card.
- **Cards.** `CARD` in `textStyles.ts`: white, rounded 22 px, a soft shadow (`shadow-card`). `CARD_LIST` is a list of rows inside one card, hairlines between rows. Course cards, Today's plan, the one change, the retry table, "Keep going" and the option lists all use them. Icon tiles are rounded on the warm tint.
- **Buttons and the action band.** `PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY` and `QUIET` are rounded (16 px) and squash slightly when pressed (`PRESS`). The action band floats: rounded, inset 16 px from the edges, a little above the tab bar, with a shadow; the action is in Fraunces semibold.
- **Masthead and tabs.** No ink rule under the title; the title is in Fraunces. The gear and the cross are 44 px rounded tiles. The current tab sits in a filled pill (around the icon on a phone, around the name on a computer).
- **Today.** The week as rounded squares that pop in one after another (green where practised, warm for today). The plan sits in a card with a bar that fills in and each step as a rounded row (the next one warm). When the day is done: a full ring, a warm badge with a tick and, the first time that day, a burst of sparks (`DoneBurst.tsx`).
- **Recording.** The bar meter is replaced by the **voice shape** (`VoiceBlob` in `RecordingScreen.tsx`): a soft shape that slowly changes outline, grows with the voice, takes the timing light's colour (warm before the window opens) and holds the clock; a dashed halo turns around it. It keeps the "Microphone level" name. The "Recording" dot is warm and breathes.
- **Results.** The numbers are tiles that count up into place (`core/countUp.ts`, `useCountUp`). A timed session shows a ring drawn round to its length against the window's end, beside the timing sentence. The one change is a card with a "One change" pill. The player is a rounded panel with a round play button.
- **Learn.** The swipe deck's segments are rounded and the current one is a little taller; Myth and Fact are rounded buttons side by side; the verdict pops in.
- **Loading** (decision 76). While saved data loads, a page shows its masthead and a few quiet rounded blocks (`Skeleton`, breathing gently when motion is welcome) and then everything at once, never a block that arrives late and pushes the rest down. Pages that read their address send the same frame (`PageFallback`) before their code arrives.
- **The voice shape is the recording control** (decisions 74 and 75). Recording screens have no bottom band. Before recording, a still 224-pixel warm shape with a microphone shows the action's words ("Start speaking", "Show my topic", "Hear the question", "Try again") and a short detail; while thinking about a prepared speech it reads "Ready"; while waiting it is a faded, non-interactive status shape ("Get ready", "Listening…", "Starting…", "Saving…"); while recording it is the live voice shape with the clock and "Stop" ("Done" in a conversation, decision 83). On screens with text to read, the shape is small and never over the text (decision 89): on a computer it sits in the margin under "Aim for…" at 112 pixels and stays in view as the text scrolls; on a phone it sits at 80 pixels in a bar at the bottom centre (paper background, a hairline above), the text takes the full width, and the page leaves room below the text for the bar. All shapes squash slightly when pressed.
- **Free talk and short speeches** (decision 73). Two more rows under "Speak on the spot". Free talk opens with "Talk about anything." and no light. A short speech is planned on its own page (topic field, numbered point fields on warm number tiles, four length buttons) and keeps its topic and numbered points in view while speaking (smaller while recording). Its results add a "Your points" card: a green tick for each point heard, a number on a quiet tile for one not heard.
- **Quick scenarios in today's practice** (decision 72). The conversation pick can be "Quick scenarios · Choose the best reply, then say it". Inside the plan the masthead reads "Step N of 4", the cross goes back to Today, and the result ends with the plan's next step instead of "Back to Learn".
- **Try it for real** (decision 71). A card's "What to do" screen can end with a white rounded panel: a globe on a warm tile, "Try it for real" in warm ink, and one small step to take away from the app.
- **Follows the device by default** (decision 89; light by default from decision 70 until then). With nothing saved the app follows the phone or computer's light or dark setting (`data-mode="system"` on the page and in the early script); Settings → Appearance lists Auto, Light and Dark, and Light or Dark is remembered.
- **Recording, calmer** (decision 77). One status line at the top (a warm dot, "Recording" or "Paused", then the timing words in muted ink, with no lamp); the question in Fraunces at cue size; the voice shape; Pause (or Resume) and Discard as two quiet buttons under it; the last two lines of words, older lines fading out through a mask. While paused the shape dims and rests smaller. A discarded take shows "Take discarded." on a white card with an Undo button for 8 seconds, and the shape reads "Start again". (The 80-pixel lane beside text to read went with decision 89; see the voice shape above.) Before speaking inside today's practice, "Can't speak now?" is a quiet underlined link at the right of the step line.
- **Results, each number once** (decision 77). The prompt, topic or question opens the page (small muted heading, Fraunces at cue size). Tiles fill one row on a phone when there are up to three. The fillers tile is a button with "Show where" in the accent; marked fillers in the transcript are dotted-underlined buttons when they can play their moment, and the heading says "Tap a marked filler to hear it."
- **Habit and home** (decision 78). Today's margin starts with "4 of the last 7 days." (and, from a week of practice, "12 practice days and counting.") above the week's squares, then the focus and "Your time: *after my morning coffee*." The plan card has no bar and no count until a step is done. **Your week** is a white card: "Your week" as a section heading, "Fillers a minute, first tries" in muted footnote, three bars on a shared scale (this week in the accent, earlier weeks in paper, a hairline when a week has none) with the number above in Fraunces and the week below, one suggestion in lede type, an optional link to act on it, and "All your history" in muted link style. The welcome hides the tab bar. Settings gains "Your practice time" (a field, five quick picks as quiet rounded buttons, a time field and "Add a daily reminder to my calendar") and "Practice days" (a switch for the practice days; called "Today" until decision 89).
- **One look everywhere** (decision 79). On a computer the masthead is one bar: the name, the tabs in pills, then the gear or cross on the right. The action band sits in place at the end of the column (`md:static`; it stuck to the bottom of the window until decision 89, and covered grids such as a result's numbers). Folded lists are `FoldCard`s: a white rounded card whose row shows the name in semibold, a muted line and a count, with a chevron that turns down when open. Switches are `CheckRow`s: a rounded box that fills with a tick, inside a 44-pixel target, with the choice and a muted line on what it means. Segmented choices (light/dark, History's All/Speaking/Reading aloud) are a paper track holding rounded buttons, the chosen one filled. Scenario replies are white rounded cards with a 2-pixel edge once answered (filled for the best, the accent for good, ink for a misstep, muted for flat). Settings sections, History's progress and the say-it panel are cards. The mission card, once taken on, is a slim warm strip with the task and "How did it go?". Learn's sets sit under "Speaking" and "People". Skills opens with "Up next" (two rows in a card), then "Everything" as folded cards.
- **Your voice** (decision 80). A "Your voice" group on Practice (Warm-up, Clear sounds, Volume, Pitch and stress, Tone, Pausing, Shadowing, each with its own icon) and a `/voice` page: the warm-up row, then one folded card per mode, including Pausing (moved from Build a skill), with "done N times" on each row; since decision 108 the tricky-word lists sit inside the Clear sounds fold, after its drills ("word list, 1 min"), not in a fold of their own. The voice shapes (`VoiceShapes.tsx`) are shared with the recording screen. **Warm-up:** the routine as three numbered warm tiles, then each move with its set in small accent type, its name in Fraunces, its cue, and a 224-pixel warm shape holding the seconds left (tap it for the next move); "Warmed up." at the end. **A drill take:** "drill · Take 1 of 3 · label" in small accent type, the line in Fraunces at statement size, the cue, then the start shape ("Record"); "One moment…" shows while the take is measured. **Results:** the headline in Fraunces; for volume and tone, one bar per take (`LevelBars`, accent on a rule-coloured track, the loudest take full width); a white card per take with its line, a quiet Play button and, for pitch and tone, a pitch line (`PitchLine`, accent, around a dashed line at the speaker's usual pitch). Stress and tone add the **guessing game** (`GuessGame`) under the headline: a white card with "Play the mystery take" and the choices as quiet buttons; the right one turns filled. Clear sounds adds a "Slow round" card. **Shadowing:** "Listen" (a speaker icon, outlined) and "Play yours" above the start shape ("Say it back"); afterwards a white card with the comparison, the pitch line, "Next phrase" and "Try it again". A muted footnote on its first page names the model voice: "an AI reading by ElevenLabs, not the original speaker" (decision 82). **Pause drills** now show a bar per mark (`PauseLengths`) with the one-to-two-second target shaded in accent tint.
- **Talk with someone** (decisions 83 and 84). A "Talk with someone" row (people icon) leads the Everyday conversation group on Practice, and each small-talk unit page has a "Try it in a conversation" row for its persona (keeping to that skill's situations). `/conversations` (one line under the title: "About 3 minutes, out loud. They talk, you answer, then tap Done.") lists the four personas on one card list: name in semibold, their tagline in muted footnote ("Quiet and thoughtful. Opens up to good questions"), how often at the right. **Before starting:** the situation's name in small accent type, "Talk with Maya" in Fraunces at statement size, the setting as a lede, a white card headed "Practise" with the skill, a muted footnote naming the AI voice, then a filled Start and, with the coach set up, a quiet "Another situation" beside it (ordinary pages never mention the passphrase; it lives in Settings' advanced section, decision 89). If the coach cannot be reached, a status line says the persona will use recorded lines here. **During:** the header shows "0:42 of 3:00" and no way out ("End conversation", a quiet button under the shape, saves instead); the person's latest line is the heading, announced politely as it changes; the accent line reads "Maya · At the office party"; the faded status shape reads "Maya / is about to speak" before the first line, then "is talking" or "is listening"; on your turn the page turns to the dark stage and the live voice shape holds your turn's clock and reads **"Done"** (`VoiceBlob`'s `action`, which is "Stop" everywhere else), with "Your turn. Tap Done when you've finished." under it. **Results** (`ConversationSessionView`): "With Theo · Before an evening class · 1:35" in accent, the headline in Fraunces ("You did 45% of the talking and asked 2 questions."), the usual One change card with "You said" (your line in quotes) and "It could sound like" added once the coach has looked, then **the conversation** as bubbles: theirs white on the left, yours warm tint on the right, each with the speaker's name in footnote semibold. The band reads "Talk again".
- **Your voice on results** (decision 81; its pitch line was replaced by pitch in the text in decision 111, below). Below the timing lines, a white card headed "Your voice" in small muted type: the take's pitch line (`PitchLine`, thinned to 240 points) with "The dashed line is your usual pitch; up is higher.", then up to four plain lines in lede size, each with a small dot (warm for something to work on, listed first; accent for what went well). A line the one change above already says is left out. When the browser kept its levelling, a muted footnote says only fading phrase ends are measured. The retry table gains a "pitch range, steps" row when both tries have one. Quiet recordings made without levelling play back louder through a gain stage set up on the first press of play.
- **Pitch with your words** (decision 111; replaces decision 81's dotted pitch line). Five views, each placed by one rule: (1) and (2) are standard on every take with pitch readings; (3) only where a text marks a key word; (4) only where the same words were said twice; (5) only on the volume drills. Colour roles only: the curve and peaks in `accent`, the usual pitch a dashed `ink-muted` line, a fall in `accent`, a hold in `ink-muted`, a rise and the key words in `warm-ink`, notes to work on in a `warm-tint` box with `warm-ink` words, the last take or the model voice a dashed `ink-muted` curve, the playing phrase on `paper-raised`, the playing word a `fill` pill. Shared pieces are in `PitchShapes.tsx` (`EndIcon`, `CurveRow`, `useRowWidth`, `useWordMeasure`); rows are at most 560 pixels wide. Every curve is drawn from the readings smoothed for drawing (`smoothForDrawing`, decision 114), so it flows like a voice rather than stepping from word to word; peaks and word heights come from the cleaned readings.
  - **(1) Pitch in the text** (`PitchText.tsx`, inside "Your voice", `VoiceSection.tsx`): one muted line on what it shows, then the take's words at lede size with wide line spacing, each lifted up to 12 pixels (2 a step) or lowered up to 8; peaks bold in the accent; a fell, held or rose mark after each phrase (read out as "ending fell"); a statement that rose has its last word in a warm-tint pill; a flat phrase muted. At most 12 phrases, then "And 8 more phrases". Up to three plain lines with dots (rising statements, flat phrases in a grey dot, where the peaks landed, or statements ending low), then the range and volume lines; "See it phrase by phrase" (filled) and Play (outlined); "Words are placed from the phone’s timings, so their places are rough." when they are. No words caught: the whole take's curve with a plain line. On the take's page of a first try and a second try.
  - **(2) Phrase rows** (`/sessions/voice?id=`, `PhraseRowsScreen.tsx`): the masthead "Your take" with a cross back; "Your voice" and one muted line; the player; a key (usual pitch, "Fell: sounds finished", "Rose: sounds like a question"); then one white card of phrases, each with "Phrase N" and its ending named ("Held: more to come"), a smooth curve over its words (wrapped into rows that fit; the spare room follows each word's length in time, so the curve keeps its timing), and a note only where useful; "0.8 s pause" between phrases on a hairline. While playing, the phrase sits on `paper-raised` ("Phrase 3 · playing"), a playhead line and ring move along its curve, and the word is a `fill` pill; each word is a 44-pixel-tall button that plays from it. 40 phrases at a time. A footnote says whether the word timings are exact (deeper analysis) or rough.
  - **(3) Planned stress** (`PlannedStressCard.tsx`, `TakeStress` in `DrillCurves.tsx`): a label ("Read aloud · planned stress", "Lesson · …", "Check-in · …"), "Did the key word get the lift?", "1 of 2 key words got your lift." over several clauses, then each clause's curve with the key words as dashed `warm-ink` circles (the word bold, dotted underline) and the highest point as an accent dot (its word bold in the accent), and one line: "Your lift landed on “she”, as planned." in the accent, or "Your lift went to “said”. Lean on “took”, and let “said” stay low." in a warm-tint box; a key under the card. On the stress lesson's results, a reading's results when it has stress marks (and the check-in's read part), and each take of the stress drill. A reading's key words are bold while reading.
  - **(4) Last try and this try** (`LastVsThisCard.tsx`): a label ("Second try · same words", "Tell it again · …", "Then and now · …"), a headline only for a clear change ("Your ending used to rise. Now it falls."), up to three matched phrases (the changed one first) with the last take's curve dashed under this one's, a key ("First try" dashed, "Second try" solid), and two half-width buttons, "Play first try" (outlined) and "Play second try" (filled), each from the shown phrase. When the last take's readings were dropped, one quiet line and this take alone. On a second try's page, a retold story's page and each check-in part with the same prompt.
  - **(5) Loudness as thickness** (`TakeThickness`): each volume-drill take's curve over its line, drawn in pieces from 2 to 10 pixels thick by how loud they were against the drill's loudest reading (one translucent `accent` layer), with "Thicker where you were louder." under the takes. Volume drills only.
  - **Shadowing** (`ShadowCurve`): your curve over the phrase's words with the model voice's dashed under it, and a key ("Model voice", "You"); "The model voice’s pitch couldn’t be read for this phrase." when it could not.
- **Focus first, and the lenses** (decision 115; the Lenses, Words, Glance and More mockups). Which views a take shows is decided by `takeViews` (`src/core/takeViews.ts`), focus first:

  | "Stress the key word" (lesson and drill p04), "Same sentence, different stress" (p05) | planned stress |
  | "Exaggerate, then dial back" (p06) | each take's curve over its words, with its range |
  | Other pitch drills, tone drills | their pitch line |
  | Volume drills | loudness as thickness |
  | Shadowing | your curve over the model voice's |
  | Clear-sounds drills | their word check |
  | Lessons "Crisp consonants", "Tongue-twister warm-up"; tricky-word lists | the clarity lens |

  | Stories | the story shape strip (and the checklist), then by length |
  | Talk with someone | the conversation timeline |
  | Everything else | by length: under 60 s and 60 to 120 s, pitch in the text; over 120 s the fingerprint at the top, the lens tabs open, "Your best moment" and the energy arc |

  **"Look closer"** (a `QUIET` button, `aria-expanded`, with the lenses it adds in muted footnote beside it) sits under the take's own view on every take with words and opens the **lens tabs** in place: pills in a scrolling row (`role="tablist"`, arrow keys move), the chosen one `fill`, the others outlined in `rule`; Clarity appears only with deeper analysis, otherwise one muted line says it needs it. Each lens is the same words in a white card at lede size with wide line spacing. **Pauses and ums:** three count tiles (between thoughts on `card`; mid-sentence on `warm-tint` with `warm-ink`; pause words), pause pills in the text (wider the longer: `accent` on a 15% `accent` tint between thoughts or for a beat before a peak; `warm-ink` on `warm-tint` with a `warm` ring mid-sentence), pause words in italic `filler` with a dotted underline, buttons that play them, a key, then up to two notes in white cards (a `warm` dot to work on, an `accent` dot for what worked). **Pace:** phrases spaced out (`tracking-[0.06em]`) when slow, drawn closer in `warm-ink` when fast, a speed line in `accent` over the usual band (15% `accent`), "start" and "end" under it, and one line. **Words:** concrete words bold in `accent`; hedges, vague words, intensifiers and the coach's swaps with a wavy `warm` underline, as buttons; a tap shows a panel in `ink` with `paper` words (the swap, or what the word does, and "Hear how you said it"). **Clarity:** the share as a line, the text muted with unsure words bold, dashed and tappable, and the drill as a `PrimaryLink` ("Practise: Clear sounds: word endings"). **At a glance** (long takes): the fingerprint card ("Your take at a glance", the headline in Fraunces at cue size, the strip in `accent`, the last stretch at 45% when it faded, `warm` ticks and marks, `ink` dots, a two-column key); "Your best moment" on `fill` with `fill-ink` words, the quote in Fraunces, a round play button in `fill-ink`; "Energy", a smooth `accent` curve over a 15% tint, the thirds divided by `rule` lines. **Story shape:** a strip of set-up (20% `accent`), turn (`fill`), end (55% `accent`) and point (`warm`), names only where a part is wide enough, the point's time, one line, and where it came from. **Conversation:** two lanes (theirs `ink-muted` at 40%, yours `accent`), `warm` dots under your questions, your share of the words and one line. **History:** each row's tiny fingerprint (112 × 22, `accent` stretches, `warm` ticks) under its day line. **Your progress:** "Your usual zone" card: per measure, its name and "132 words/min · inside your usual", a `rule` track, the usual band at 25% `accent` and the latest take as an `accent` dot, the same colour whatever the side.
- **Rate yourself before the results** (decision 116). After a take of Rapid Fire, a prepared speech, free talk, a short speech, a story, reading aloud (not a tricky-word list) or a check-in part is saved, and before its results, the recording page shows one question on its own (`SelfRatingAsk.tsx`): "Saved. Before your results" in small accent type, "How did that feel?" in Fraunces at statement size, three answers as one row of equal rounded buttons on `card` with a `rule` edge (Rushed, About right, Slow; for a story Flat, OK, Lively), a `QUIET` "Skip" under them, and one muted footnote on what happens next. One tap saves the answer and opens the results; Skip opens them at once. If the answer cannot be saved, a line says so ("Your answer could not be saved. Your take is saved.") with a `SECONDARY` "See your results". Voice drills, pause drills, lessons, small talk, rehearsals and moment notes are never asked; nor are the first two rounds of a 4/3/2 loop. **On the results**, one line in lede type under the headline (above the One change card; on a second try under "You were working on"): "You said rushed. Your pace was 150 a minute, inside your usual." It shows only when the measure is confident (`compareRating` in `src/core/ratingCompare.ts`), otherwise nothing; no colour, no chart, decision 103's neutral words. **On Your progress**, after Check-ins, one small white card with a line per scale once eight rated takes could be compared: "Your sense of your pace matched the measure in 7 of your last 10 takes."
- **Goals and two intonation drills** (decision 117). **The goal line:** before the first take of every voice drill, on a shadowing passage's first page, and before starting a lesson, a pause drill or a tricky-word list, one footnote line: "**Goal:**" in semibold, then what good looks like and how it will be shown ("Your highest pitch lands on the key word. You’ll see a circle on the target and a dot where your peak landed."), `data-goal`, in ink (not muted, so it reads as part of the task). On a drill it sits under the intro line; on a lesson under the tip; on shadowing under a shorter how-it-works line. **Same sentence, different stress** (p05) and **Exaggerate, then dial back** (p06) are rows in the Pitch and stress fold ("6 sentences"); the take label adds "· Sentence 2 of 6", and before the first take a footnote text link "Another sentence". Results: for the stress shift, each take's card holds the planned-stress row (decision 111: the target as a dashed `warm-ink` circle, the highest point as an `accent` dot) with "Peak on “she”, the target." or "Peak on “say”, not “she”." and a key line under the cards; the headline counts the hits. For dial back, each take's card holds its curve over its words (`TakeCurve`, the phrase row's `CurveRow`) and "Range: 8 steps."; the headline is the one line on the share kept; a footnote says the dashed line is each take's own usual pitch. No new chart types; colour roles only.
- **Tell it three times, shorter each time** (decision 118, the 4/3/2 loop). **The offer:** before a prepared speech or a story, a text link under the start shape, "Tell it three times, shorter each time", with the times in muted footnote beside it ("4:00, then 3:00, then 2:00"); on a story's set-up page a `CheckRow` with the same words and the times as its detail, after "How long?", which turns the band's aside into "three rounds, one sitting". **A round:** an accent footnote line under the masthead, "Told three times · round 2 of 3 · 3:00", adding "Same content, less time." before a later round starts; the start shape reads "Start round 2" with "3:00 this round"; the timing light and its words follow the round's own window. **After round 3** (the rating question first, decision 116), under the headline: a small muted label "Told three times · 4/3/2", "Your three rounds" as a section heading, one line in lede type on what changed, then the phrase-row card (last try and this try's look) with round 3's curve solid in `accent`, round 2's dashed `ink-muted` and round 1's dotted `ink-muted` at 45%, a key "Round 1 · Round 2 · Round 3", then a white card with a compact table (rounds as columns; pace, pause words, pauses, story checks for a story, time / target), tabular figures, scrolling inside itself on a narrow phone. An earlier round's page has one muted footnote, "Round 1 of 3, told three times." with "See all three rounds".
- **Rounds 4 and 5** (decision 89). **Room under the band:** on a phone every page with a floating action band leaves its height (`--vl-band-h`, 6rem with the gap) plus the tab bar below the last content, set once in `globals.css`, so nothing is left under it at the end of the page. **A take not kept** (too short, nothing heard, could not save) is a warm-tint card first in the column, above any text to read, scrolled to and focused; Rapid Fire keeps the topic in view under "Same topic for your next try". **Small-talk results** ask "Which beats did you hit?" with the ticks open under the question until the user ticks or the coach reports, then show the count. **A result's day** reads "Recorded today" as a small label beside the gear. **History** has "‹ Back to Settings" and a `PageTitle` ("Settings" in the masthead), the progress sentence as a second-level heading. **Today** leaves out its margin when it has nothing to say (one centred column) and the focus line until there is practice; its total is the steps' minutes added up. **Skip link:** "Skip to content" is the first thing on every page, hidden until focused, then a filled rounded tile in the top-left corner; it moves focus to the main heading. **Numbers:** the body font's zero is drawn without a slash (`src/theme/fonts/AtkinsonHyperlegibleNext-OpenZero.woff2`). **Screen readers:** a countdown's number is not a live region (a hidden status speaks at the start and the last three seconds); a status line is named by its own words; a coloured dot carries its meaning in hidden words.
- **Your speaking profile** (decision 90; the one "how am I doing" place since decision 109). `/profile`, reached from the one row under "Your progress" in Settings ("Your speaking profile and history"); it marks the gear and is not a tab. The masthead says "Your progress" with "Back to Settings"; History is reached from a text link at its end ("Every session, in History") and from Today, and its own masthead also says "Your progress", with "Back to your progress"; the title "Your speaking profile" has one muted line ("Your usual against your recent takes. Compared only with yourself."). The latest weekly coach summary is a white card at the top: a small muted label with its day, the headline in Fraunces at cue size, "*Next week.* …", and a fold "More from the coach" (improvements, the pattern, a practice to try). Then a **Check-ins** section heading over a white card: "Latest · N so far" in a small muted label, the latest check-in's day in Fraunces, the latest six days as pills, and "Compare first and latest" (from two) and "All check-ins"; before the first, one line on what a check-in is and "Your first check-in". - **Motion** (`globals.css`): pop, rise, fill, burst, morph, slow spin, breathe and ring. Every one is used through `motion-safe:`, so with reduced motion the end state shows at once and nothing moves; counting up also starts at the final numbers.

## Direction: the script page (chosen 2026-10-13)

VoiceLab reads like a **printed speech script or a magazine page**, not an app dashboard. It was chosen over a tape-recorder look and a monospaced "instrument" look after the first wireframes read as template-made. One idea is borrowed from the tape recorder: a live level meter while recording.

Rules for every screen:

- **Serif type throughout** (Newsreader). The daily prompt is set like a headline, with a hanging opening quote in the accent.
- **Prose instead of widgets.** Streak, focus, and progress are written as sentences ("Fourth day running."). Numbers appear inside sentences ("From 4.2 to 1.8 fillers a minute."); the one exception is the compact row of numbers on a session's page.
- **Main navigation** (changed 2026-10-18, decision 66; was four tabs, decisions 48 and 56, and five then six before that, decisions 41, 42 and 45): three tabs, Today · Practice · Learn. Connect's mission moved to the top of Learn and its units live under Practice. History is a folded section at the top of Settings ("Show your practice history"), drawn only once opened; the `/history` page marks the gear. On a phone it is a tab bar fixed to the bottom of the screen, an icon over a small label, the current section filled in the accent. On a computer it is a row of tabs above the masthead, the current one underlined in the accent. Settings is a gear at the right of the masthead line (filled on the settings page). Skills, the small-talk units and their sessions mark the Practice tab. A session's results mark the tab it belongs to: Today inside today's practice, otherwise Practice (`src/core/sessionHome.ts`).
- **Practice** (decision 48; slimmed in decision 108). The page title "Practice" and one muted line, then four groups under section headings (the focus's groups first, marked "Your focus"), each a ruled list of full-width rows: the icon on a faint accent tile, the mode's name in the display face over a one-line footnote, and a chevron. **Each group shows three rows (four for a focus group), the most relevant first, and "See all" for the rest**: a link to the page that holds them (Tell stories → `/stories`, Everyday conversation → `/small-talk`, Your voice → `/voice`), or, for Speak on the spot, which has no page of its own, a button beside the heading that opens the rest in place (`aria-expanded`, "Show fewer" to close). Speak on the spot shows Rapid Fire, Prepared speech and Read aloud, with Free talk and Short speech under "See all" (with speaking at work or a talk coming up as a focus, Your talk and Short speech come first). Tell stories shows Today's story, Tell one again and Guided stories. Your voice shows the warm-up, then the modes done least (clear sounds, which now holds the tricky-word lists, volume, pitch and stress, tone, pausing, shadowing), each row starting its next drill ("Next: …"). Every mode is within two taps. The weekly challenge follows. There is no "Build a skill" group (the lessons are in Learn's Speaking skills set, decision 107) and no Openings and closings (decision 106). A mode that cannot start yet would be a muted row without a link or chevron (none today).
- **Settings** (grouped in decision 110). The page title, then five headings in the display face at 2xl, each over its white cards (each card a small run-in heading and its controls): **Your progress** (one row, "Your speaking profile and history"); **Practice** (What you want to get better at, Your practice time, Weekly check-in, Conversation turns, then one folded card, "More: practice days, pause words, cues while speaking", whose sections are ruled blocks inside it); **Recordings and data** (Your recordings: the days kept, the check-in keep rule and the starred-take note; Your data: export, import and whether the browser may clear it; Start over); **Coach and services** (Coach, with its setup folded and this month's conversation minutes when a passphrase is saved; Deeper analysis); **Appearance** (Light or dark). ## Roles, not raw values

Components never use a raw color, font, or size. They use a **role** (a named purpose), and each theme fills in every role.

| Role | Used for |
|------|----------|
| `paper` | Page background |
| `paper-raised` | Slightly lifted surfaces (sheets, the record panel) |
| `ink` | Main text |
| `ink-muted` | Secondary text, captions |
| `rule` | Thin dividing lines and borders |
| `accent` | The record button and "your one fix" only |
| `accent-ink` | Text placed on the accent |
| `fill` | Large filled areas: the action band, primary buttons, chosen answers (the accent in light mode; a deeper green in dark mode, so big areas are not the brightest thing on a dark screen; decision 59) |
| `fill-ink` | Text placed on `fill` |
| `filler` | Filler words in a transcript: gray text on a faint gray highlight, never red and never underlined (an underline reads as a link) |
| `focus-ring` | Keyboard focus outline |
| `light-green` | Rapid Fire timing light from 1:00: the minimum is met |
| `light-yellow` | Rapid Fire timing light from 1:30: start wrapping up |
| `light-red` | Rapid Fire timing light from 2:00: finish now (stays on; past 2:30 the lamp gains a heavy ring) |

Fonts use two roles: `font-body` and `font-display` (headings and the daily prompt).

Tailwind's built-in color palette is switched off, so a class like `bg-blue-500` does not exist. Only role classes such as `bg-paper` and `text-ink` work.

## Where things live

| File | What it holds |
|------|---------------|
| `src/theme/themes.ts` | **Every color and font stack.** To change the look, edit this one file |
| `src/theme/roles.ts` | The role list and the contrast rules every theme must pass |
| `src/theme/fonts.ts` | Loads the web fonts (downloaded at build time and served from our own site, so no font requests go to Google from the user's phone) |
| `src/theme/themeCss.ts` | Turns the themes into CSS variables, switched by `data-theme` (and `data-mode` to force light or dark) |
| `src/app/globals.css` | Exposes each role to Tailwind (`bg-paper`, `text-ink`, ...) and switches off Tailwind's built-in palette |

Dark mode follows the phone's setting automatically.

## Shape and layout

- Flat surfaces separated by thin rules, not shadowed cards. Square corners. Where a page offers a set of choices, each may be a flat card (see "Cards for choices").
- Phone first. One main action per screen, generous spacing.
- Transcripts read like notes; fillers are marked quietly, not as errors.

## Tests that guard this

- Every theme defines every role.
- Every text/background pair in every theme passes WCAG AA contrast (risk R13).
- No file in `src/` outside `src/theme/` contains a raw color, a color function, a built-in Tailwind color class, an arbitrary color or font value, or an inline font.
- Page-wide defaults read theme variables directly, so a themed area inside another (as the removed style page's previews did) gets its own colors.
