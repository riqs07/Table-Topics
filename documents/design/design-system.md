# Design System

**Purpose:** How you control colors, fonts, and feel, and the rules that keep the look in one place.
**Last updated:** 2026-10-19
**Status:** Built in milestone 2. **Calm studio with a forest green accent was chosen at C6 (2026-10-12).** All three themes stay on the style page (`/style`) for comparison.

## Warm and lively (decision 69, 2026-10-19)

The user found the script page stiff and asked for something more fun. These rules sit on top of the script page below and win wherever the two disagree (the older rules are kept for their history).

- **Type.** Headlines, page titles, section headings, card names, the action band and every big number are in **Fraunces** (semibold, slightly tight); running text is **Atkinson Hyperlegible**. Both are set by the calm studio theme (`src/theme/fonts.ts`, `themes.ts`).
- **New colour roles**: `card` (white panels lifted off the page), `warm` (the apricot accent: today's square, the next step's number, the done badge, the live dot), `warm-tint` (its soft background: the next step's row, the mission card, icon tiles, the "One change" pill), `warm-ink` (text on the tint) and `on-warm` (text and ticks on `warm`). The contrast test covers every pair they are used in.
- **Areas** (`data-area`). Three parts of the app get their own surfaces, defined per theme in `themes.areas` and written by `themeCss.ts`; while one is showing, the whole page takes its colours so the tab bar and edges match:
  - `stage`: deep green with light ink while the clock runs (thinking and recording).
  - `results`: a soft green on the session page.
  - `learn`: sand on Learn, a set's page and a card.
- **Cards.** `CARD` in `textStyles.ts`: white, rounded 22 px, a soft shadow (`shadow-card`). `CARD_LIST` is a list of rows inside one card, hairlines between rows. Course cards, Today's plan, the one change, the retry table, "Keep going" and the option lists all use them. Icon tiles are rounded on the warm tint.
- **Buttons and the action band.** `PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY` and `QUIET` are rounded (16 px) and squash slightly when pressed (`PRESS`). The action band floats: rounded, inset 16 px from the edges, a little above the tab bar, with a shadow; the action is in Fraunces semibold.
- **Masthead and tabs.** No ink rule under the title; the title is in Fraunces. The gear and the cross are 44 px rounded tiles. The current tab sits in a filled pill (around the icon on a phone, around the name on a computer).
- **Today.** The week as rounded squares that pop in one after another (green where practised, warm for today). The plan sits in a card with a bar that fills in and each step as a rounded row (the next one warm). When the day is done: a full ring, a warm badge with a tick and, the first time that day, a burst of sparks (`DoneBurst.tsx`).
- **Recording.** The bar meter is replaced by the **voice shape** (`VoiceBlob` in `RecordingScreen.tsx`): a soft shape that slowly changes outline, grows with the voice, takes the timing light's colour (warm before the window opens) and holds the clock; a dashed halo turns around it. It keeps the "Microphone level" name. The "Recording" dot is warm and breathes.
- **Results.** The numbers are tiles that count up into place (`core/countUp.ts`, `useCountUp`). A timed session shows a ring drawn round to its length against the window's end, beside the timing sentence. The one change is a card with a "One change" pill. The player is a rounded panel with a round play button.
- **Learn.** The swipe deck's segments are rounded and the current one is a little taller; Myth and Fact are rounded buttons side by side; the verdict pops in.
- **Quick scenarios in today's practice** (decision 72). The conversation pick can be "Quick scenarios · Choose the best reply, then say it". Inside the plan the masthead reads "Step N of 4", the cross goes back to Today, and the result ends with the plan's next step instead of "Back to Learn".
- **Try it for real** (decision 71). A card's "What to do" screen can end with a white rounded panel: a globe on a warm tile, "Try it for real" in warm ink, and one small step to take away from the app.
- **Light by default** (decision 70). The app is light unless the user picks Dark or Auto in Settings → Appearance; Auto follows the phone or computer.
- **Motion** (`globals.css`): pop, rise, fill, burst, morph, slow spin, breathe and ring. Every one is used through `motion-safe:`, so with reduced motion the end state shows at once and nothing moves; counting up also starts at the final numbers.

## Direction: the script page (chosen 2026-10-13)

VoiceLab reads like a **printed speech script or a magazine page**, not an app dashboard. It was chosen over a tape-recorder look and a monospaced "instrument" look after the first wireframes read as template-made. One idea is borrowed from the tape recorder: a live level meter while recording.

Rules for every screen:

- **Serif type throughout** (Newsreader). The daily prompt is set like a headline, with a hanging opening quote in the accent.
- **Prose instead of widgets.** Streak, focus, and progress are written as sentences ("Fourth day running."). Numbers appear inside sentences ("From 4.2 to 1.8 fillers a minute."); the one exception is the compact row of numbers on a session's page.
- **Main navigation** (changed 2026-10-18, decision 66; was four tabs, decisions 48 and 56, and five then six before that, decisions 41, 42 and 45): three tabs, Today · Practice · Learn. Connect's mission moved to the top of Learn and its units live under Practice. History is a folded section at the top of Settings ("Show your practice history"), drawn only once opened; the `/history` page marks the gear. On a phone it is a tab bar fixed to the bottom of the screen, an icon over a small label, the current section filled in the accent. On a computer it is a row of tabs above the masthead, the current one underlined in the accent. Settings is a gear at the right of the masthead line (filled on the settings page). Skills, the small-talk units and their sessions mark the Practice tab. A session's results mark the tab it belongs to: Today inside today's practice, otherwise Practice (`src/core/sessionHome.ts`).
- **Practice** (decision 48). The page title "Practice" and one muted line, then groups under section headings, each a ruled list of full-width rows: the icon on a faint accent tile (as on the course cards), the mode's name in the display face over a one-line footnote, and a chevron. A mode that cannot start yet would be a muted row without a link or chevron (none today; Q&A practice, the only one, was removed in decision 61). The weekly challenge follows (Your talks are hidden for now, decision 49). Lessons, Pause drills and Tricky words start the next one straight away, with "Next: …" as their line; "Build a skill" has a "See all" link to the full Skills page; Read aloud still opens the list of readings (decision 67).
- **Focus mode while practising** (added 2026-10-17, decision 41). The recording page and the listening step have no tab bar and no gear; a cross at the right of the masthead line leads back to where the session belongs ("Close, back to Practice"), and it too goes while recording, counting down or saving (discarding is the way out then). Keyboard hints in the masthead show only on devices with a fine pointer (`pointer-fine:`). Before a reading starts on a phone, the text comes first: the big clock is hidden and the length sits small in the masthead line until recording starts. Inside today's practice the masthead note is "Step 1 of 2" on a phone and "Today's practice · step 1 of 2" on a computer.
- **A masthead line** on every screen: title in small capitals on the left, a quiet note on the right (then the gear or the cross), a solid hairline underneath. The four tab pages and Settings show "VoiceLab" there and put the page's name in a large `PageTitle` below it (the name, then at most one short muted line), so the name is never said twice (decision 59). Pages inside a tab show the tab's name ("Practice" over "Build a skill").
- **On a computer** (decision 65). Every page with the main navigation uses `PAGE`: the tabs and masthead keep one width (`max-w-5xl`) and the content under them is at most `max-w-3xl`, so rows keep their chevrons close; the action band stays `max-w-md` with room above it. A session's results put the notes in the wide left column and the transcript on the right; a card's Back and Next sit at the right.
- **Few words** (decision 63). A screen says what to do. Reasons, evidence, how a mode works and what comes afterwards are one tap away (a `details` fold such as "Tip and why", the Learn card, the lesson itself) or cut. Lists show titles, with at most a short count or status once there is one. No notes explaining the screen itself, no instructions for using a control, and nothing said twice. A browser check (`tests/e2e/wordBudget.spec.ts`) keeps the first phone screen of the main pages at 100 words or fewer.
- **Keeping practice safe** (decision 64). Today shows at most one reminder, below the steps: a short line with an accent rule on the left ("Add VoiceLab to your Home Screen…" on iPhone Safari, or "Save a copy of your practice now and then." with a "Save a copy" link to Settings), and a quiet "Not now". Settings › Your data adds one muted line on whether the browser will keep the data and when the last copy was saved.
- **Section headings** use `SECTION_HEADING` (`src/components/script/textStyles.ts`): the display face, a size up from body text, upright. **Labels** over a block ("Today's mission · Follow up", "Learn · 1 min") are small muted text, upright. Italic is kept for quotes, the prompt reminder while recording and the odd emphasised word, never for labels, hints or actions (decision 59; before it, run-in headings and labels were all italic).
- **Buttons come from one place** (`src/components/script/Buttons.tsx`, decision 59): `PRIMARY` (filled, `fill` role), `SECONDARY` (outlined in ink), `QUIET` (outlined in `rule`, muted words: "Not today", "Skip this time", "Discard this one", "Back") and `TEXT_LINK` (underlined words for going to another page or section). A button that cannot be used yet turns solid `rule` gray with muted words; nothing is ever faded with opacity, so nothing shows through. New buttons use these names, never a hand-written class list.
- **One full-width action band** for the main action, square-cornered, with the action large in the display face (upright since decision 59). On a phone it is fixed to the bottom of the screen, just above the tab bar, so it is always in reach (`ActionBand`, `ActionButton`; the page leaves room for it); it is 64 pixels tall (80 before decision 41). Secondary actions are footnotes above it, except on a session's results, where they are a short list of full-width rows with chevrons ("Read what I said", "Read part 2", "Try it again", "Finish for today"; `RowLink`, `RowButton`) straight after the content, with no empty space pushing them down.
- **Recording looks like recording** (added 2026-10-16). While the microphone is on, the Stop band turns ink (`ActionButton variant="stop"`), a red dot marked "Recording" pulses under the clock (still when the user asks for reduced motion), the clock grows to `text-prompt-xl`, and the timing lamp and level meter are larger. The prompt stays in view above any how-this-works note, so the note never pushes the prompt off a phone screen. The level meter is a row of thin rounded bars growing from a centre line, like a waveform (decision 59). Before the timing light comes on, its lamp is a dashed ring and says when it will light ("Turns green at 1:00"). "Leave without speaking" and "Discard this one" are quiet buttons. Before a Table Topic, the briefing says once how it works ("Your topic is hidden until you start.") and the separate how-this-works note is left out.
- **A session's page: one results screen** (added 2026-10-16; since decision 67 the only screen after a recording). It opens at once, with no listening step and nothing to fill in first. The focus number as a headline when one measures it (otherwise no headline: the focus's bare name read like praise); then the one change and the cue for the next try, together in a block with a thick accent rule on the left ("From your numbers" until the coach's note arrives, decision 40); then the player ("then and now" when there is an earlier day's take); then the numbers in one compact row (`src/core/resultNumbers.ts`) and the timing and pace lines. Under them, "Add your notes": a fold with the quick tags ("Worked" and "To change"; for small talk, the beats to tick) and one optional line, saved with a secondary "Save my notes"; once saved, "Your notes" and the notes against the numbers take its place. When the coach is set to read notes, the fold opens by itself and offers "Get feedback without notes". The rows end with a way back to where the session started ("Back to Practice", "Back to Answering"), never "Finish for today"; the band is "Try it again", or inside today's practice the next step. Recordings are kept within the retention setting (the per-take "keep this recording" choice went with the listening step).
- **Recordings play from the app's own player** (`src/components/script/Player.tsx`): one large square play button in the accent, a bar to move through the recording, and the time against the length that was saved with the session, because recorded audio often cannot report its own length.
- **Progress you can see at a glance** (added 2026-10-16). Today shows the last seven days as seven squares under the streak sentence, filled in the accent where there was practice, with today outlined (`src/core/weekStrip.ts`). The lessons on Skills read as a path: a filled square with a tick for a lesson done, the number in an accent outline with "Next" above the title for the first lesson not yet done, a quiet outline for the rest, joined by a thin line (`src/core/lessonPath.ts`). The trend on History has a caption saying what it measures, and its first and latest values at either end; a flat trend sits in the middle, not along the top edge.
- **An empty page offers one thing to do**, as a filled button: History's empty state leads to "Record your first session".
- **Cards for choices** (added 2026-10-16, decision 27; relaxes the earlier "no cards" rule). Where a page offers several things to choose between, each is a card: square corners, a hairline edge in `rule`, the plain `paper` behind it, no shadow (`src/components/script/Card.tsx`). The Practice modes are cards with an accent icon at the top and their start button at the bottom; the famous speeches on Skills are cards showing the title and who gave it. Lists of many small items (lessons, practice passages, history) stay as ruled rows.
- **Reading along lights up the text** (added 2026-10-16, decision 28). While recording with text on screen (readings, lessons, pause drills, tricky words, rehearsals), the words already read are in full ink, the next word has a faint accent highlight, and the words ahead are dimmed to `ink-muted`. It shows progress only: a skipped or misheard word is never marked during reading, so the reader does not lose their place. Missed words appear afterwards on the session page. The page scrolls to keep the reading line about a third of the way down, without animation when the user asks for reduced motion. If recognition lags, the scroll keeps moving at a reading pace while the microphone hears speech, up to 30 words past the last recognised word (decision 58); the highlight itself never runs ahead.
- **Listen back first** (decision 30; replaced by decision 67). The separate listening step, with its full-listen lock, two boxes and eleven tags, is gone: the player, the tags and one optional line now sit on the results screen.
- **Whole speeches, a part at a time** (added 2026-10-17, decision 33). On Skills, readings come in three groups of flat cards: whole speeches, commencement speeches (each card: title, speaker and year, number of parts and total length, and how many parts are read) and short readings (with their length). Every reading shows its length in words people use ("about 1½ minutes", `src/core/readingLength.ts`). A speech's own page lists its parts as a path with done and next marks (the same `PathMark` as the lessons), each with its opening words and length, and the action band reads the next part. Parts are about a minute each, so one round of read, listen back and try again takes about three minutes. After reading a part, the results page offers "Read part N".
- **Today's practice** (added 2026-10-17, decision 34; since decision 51 four steps: the Learn card ("Learn"; none once every card is read, decision 67), "Warm up", then two picks labelled by what they are, such as "Table Topic" or "Small-talk lesson 2"). Once every step is done, "Keep going" follows the plan under a section heading: up to four ruled rows, each with its kind in small muted type over what it uses, its minutes on the right, and a chevron. Today leads with the day's plan: a small line with the total time, a headline that says where things stand ("Four short steps.", "Pick up where you left off.", "Done for today."), and the steps as a path with the same done and next marks as the lessons, each with its name, minutes and what it uses, and once done the user's own note or how long they spoke (never a filler count on Today). When every step is done, the headline "Done for today." is followed by the time spoken today and which day of the week this is ("You spoke for 3 minutes 20 today. Fourth day this week."), and the first time Today shows it that day, the ticks grow in one by one (`motion-safe:animate-tick-in`; still under reduced motion; decision 39). The action band starts or continues the plan, and after the last step offers "Practise more", which opens Practice. Nothing else sits below the plan: practising outside it is the Practice tab's job (decision 48; the "Or practise on your own" tiles are gone). Today keeps only a short streak line, the seven-day strip and "This week: pausing." beside them; the weekly challenge lives at the foot of Practice, and the three jobs list is gone (the tab bar covers it). Decluttered 2026-10-17 (decision 36). A first visit's welcome is a greeting and one "Start" into step 1 (decision 67; the "what brings you here?" question is gone). Inside the plan, the recording page says "Today's practice · step 2 of 3" under the masthead, the session pages put it in the masthead's corner, and their action band is "Next: step 3" or "Finish today's practice". On the listening step that band is open from the start (listening is encouraged, not required), and "Try it again" and "Save and see the feedback" sit above it as links.
- **The microphone is explained first** (added 2026-10-17, decision 38). Before the first ever recording, if the browser is about to ask, the recording page gives way to one plain screen: the headline "VoiceLab listens through your microphone.", one line on privacy and what comes next, "Not now" just under the words (never at the foot, where the band would cover it), and "Allow microphone" in the action band. Problems on the recording page (a blocked microphone, no speech service) sit first in the main column, above any text to read; a blocked microphone lists the steps to allow it per device.
- **A reading stopped early is not a failure** (added 2026-10-17, decision 39). When a reading stops well before the end (at least 8 words and a tenth of the text left after the last word heard), the session page says "You read the first 112 words." instead of a low percentage, counts words "not heard in that stretch" only up to there, and shows the rest of the text dimmed rather than struck through.
- **Small talk** (added 2026-10-17, decision 42). A fifth tab, "Small talk", with a chat icon. Its page leads with "Say more than “good.”", then today's question between two ink rules, the recipe as a numbered list (each beat's name in the display face, its reminder, and an italic line on why it works), the lessons as a path with done and next marks, the everyday questions folded by group, and your answers with "3 of 4" on the right. On the recording page a lesson shows its idea, then a flat answer (quiet rule on the left) above a better one (accent rule), then "Your turn" and the question set like a headline. Under the question sits the recipe strip: the four beats two by two on a phone and in a row on a computer, the lesson's beat marked by an accent rule; reminders shrink to the bare word once a beat is a habit and the strip hides behind "Show the recipe" once all four are. Listening back lists the four beats as full-width switches with their questions. The notes lead with "3 of 4 beats." and a row per beat: a filled square with a tick and the speaker's words in quotes when found, an empty square and the reminder when not. The coach's livelier version sits in the accent block, with its bracketed gaps in italic accent. "One moment a day" (decision 44) sits under today's question on the Small talk page: a sentence that changes once today's moment is saved, a link to record one, and the latest moments as ruled rows (the day in small italic, then the words). Beside a question about what you have been up to, this week's moments appear under the recipe as quiet indented lines headed "From your moments this week".
- **Scenario questions** (decision 54). The situation in body text, "What’s the best thing to say?" in italic, then the replies as full-width outlined buttons. Once one is chosen, every reply shows its tier and pattern in small italic under its words: the best filled in the accent, "Also good" with an accent edge, "Flat" with a rule edge, "Misstep" with an ink edge (the tier is always in words). Then a display-face verdict, the reasons, "The move" with an accent rule on the left, any context note in small italic, and "Now say it in your own words" with the recorder. Quick scenarios run one at a time with "1 of 3" in the masthead, ending on "2 of 3 best." and a link to today's mission.
- **Missions** (decision 52; on Today since decision 62 only the small heading, the task and "Take it on in Learn", until it is accepted; the full card leads Learn since decision 66). A card between ink rules: "Today's mission · Follow up" in small italic, the task in the display face, the habit to drop with an accent rule on the left, the reason in a footnote with a "Why it works" link, then "How will it go?" and a row of five equal squares with the number only, "Badly" and "Very well" under the two ends (each square still says its word to screen readers; the habit to drop and the reason are folded behind "Tip and why", decision 63), "I'll try it" (primary, solid gray until a prediction is chosen) and "Not today" (quiet). The scale's words sit small and on one line under each number. Waiting: "How did it go?" as an accent button. The debrief page is focus mode with a close cross; after saving it leads with the comparison sentence. Connect lists the latest tries with two short bars each, "said" in the rule colour over "went" in the accent.
- **Course cards** (decision 57; quieter since decision 59). Learn's sets are cards (Connect's unit cards went with Connect, decision 66): a hairline-edged paper panel, the whole card a link, with an icon in the accent on a faint accent tile (48 pixels), the name in the display face, one muted line, then how many there are ("6 cards") until one is done, and only then a thin progress bar filled in the accent with "3/6" beside it (a page of empty bars read as a template); "Next up" in small accent type above the next one. Two columns of equal height on a computer. A set or unit page opens with "‹ All sets" or "‹ Connect", then the faint icon tile beside the name, then its content.
- **Small talk's units** (added 2026-10-18, decision 47; since decision 57 each unit has its own page reached from its row on Practice (from its card on Connect until decision 66), replacing the switches). Under the back link and the unit's icon and name, an italic line says when the unit is for. The practice box, recipe, lessons path and list below follow the unit; moments and the everyday questions show under Answering only, "Things people say" under Asking. On the recording page, asking shows a small italic "They say" above the remark set like a headline; listening shows "Listen to their story." as the headline and one line on why it is hidden (a lesson shows its story at statement size). Lessons in these units show "They say" and the other person's line before the flat and better replies. The results lead with "They said" or "Their story" in small italic and the words in quotes, then the beats, and the accent block ends with "A better follow-up" or "A better response".
- **Learn** (added 2026-10-18, decision 45; tab and page since decisions 57, 66 and 67). The Learn page leads with today's mission, then Quick scenarios and the sets as course cards. A card is focus mode (no tab bar; the set's name in the masthead and a cross back) with four screens side by side that snap into place when swiped: the question or belief (with a myth-or-fact guess), what the research found (with its evidence label in words and its source), what it means, and what to do (with a short recorded exercise on try-it cards); cards with a scenario add it as a fifth, last screen. Segments across the top show where you are; at the foot, a quiet Back beside a primary Next, which on the last screen becomes the way on: "Next card", "Back to" the set after the last card, or inside today's practice "Next: step 2" or "Finish today's practice". Reaching the last screen marks the card read. There is no recall quiz and no review (decision 67).
- **Tap targets are at least 44 pixels tall on a phone** (added 2026-10-16). Inside a page, use the shared styles in `src/components/script/Buttons.tsx`: `PrimaryLink` (filled accent), `SecondaryLink` (outlined in ink), and `RowLink` (a whole row with a chevron). A browser check measures every control on every main page; links inside running sentences carry `data-inline` and are exempt.
- **Footnotes** (a thin rule, smaller text) for anything secondary: the weekly challenge, "or choose another", "Listen to your first recording."
- **Left-aligned, square edges, hairline rules.** Lists use dotted leaders like a table of contents.

Never: uppercase letter-spaced micro-labels, centered stacks of widgets, emoji, gradients, or cheerleading copy. (Rounded and shadowed cards, rounded buttons and the tab pill have been allowed since decision 69.) A test fails if the wireframes use pill buttons or uppercase labels.

Type sizes are named in `src/app/globals.css` (`text-footnote`, `text-lede`, `text-running`, `text-cue`, `text-band`, `text-statement`, `text-prompt`); components use the names, not pixel values.

## Tools

| Tool | Job | Why |
|------|-----|-----|
| Tailwind CSS v4 | Styling | Theme values are CSS variables in one file, so a palette change is one edit |
| React Aria Components (added in milestone 7) | Buttons, dialogs, switches | Accessible and phone-friendly with no visual style of its own |
| Phosphor Icons | Icons | Less common than the default icon sets; has light and regular weights |

## Roles, not raw values

Components never use a raw color, font, or size. They use a **role** (a named purpose), and each theme fills in every role.

| Role | Used for |
|------|----------|
| `paper` | Page background |
| `paper-raised` | Slightly lifted surfaces (sheets, the record panel) |
| `ink` | Main text |
| `ink-muted` | Secondary text, captions |
| `rule` | Thin dividing lines and borders |
| `accent` | The record button and "your one fix" only |
| `accent-ink` | Text placed on the accent |
| `fill` | Large filled areas: the action band, primary buttons, chosen answers (the accent in light mode; a deeper green in dark mode, so big areas are not the brightest thing on a dark screen; decision 59) |
| `fill-ink` | Text placed on `fill` |
| `filler` | Filler words in a transcript: gray text on a faint gray highlight, never red and never underlined (an underline reads as a link) |
| `focus-ring` | Keyboard focus outline |
| `light-green` | Table Topics timing light from 1:00: the minimum is met |
| `light-yellow` | Table Topics timing light from 1:30: start wrapping up |
| `light-red` | Table Topics timing light from 2:00: finish now (stays on; past 2:30 the lamp gains a heavy ring) |

Fonts use two roles: `font-body` and `font-display` (headings and the daily prompt).

Tailwind's built-in color palette is switched off, so a class like `bg-blue-500` does not exist. Only role classes such as `bg-paper` and `text-ink` work.

## Starter themes

| Theme | Feel | Fonts |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Notebook** | Warm off-white paper, ink text, one terracotta accent; warm charcoal in dark mode | Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (body), Young Serif (display) |

## Where things live

| File | What it holds |
|------|---------------|
| `src/theme/themes.ts` | **Every color and font stack.** To change the look, edit this one file |
| `src/theme/roles.ts` | The role list and the contrast rules every theme must pass |
| `src/theme/fonts.ts` | Loads the web fonts (downloaded at build time and served from our own site, so no font requests go to Google from the user's phone) |
| `src/theme/themeCss.ts` | Turns the themes into CSS variables, switched by `data-theme` (and `data-mode` to force light or dark) |
| `src/app/globals.css` | Exposes each role to Tailwind (`bg-paper`, `text-ink`, ...) and switches off Tailwind's built-in palette |

Dark mode follows the phone's setting automatically.

## Shape and layout

- Flat surfaces separated by thin rules, not shadowed cards. Square corners. Where a page offers a set of choices, each may be a flat card (see "Cards for choices").
- Phone first. One main action per screen, generous spacing.
- Transcripts read like notes; fillers are marked quietly, not as errors.

## Tests that guard this

- Every theme defines every role.
- Every text/background pair in every theme passes WCAG AA contrast (risk R13).
- No file in `src/` outside `src/theme/` contains a raw color, a color function, a built-in Tailwind color class, an arbitrary color or font value, or an inline font.
- Page-wide defaults read theme variables directly, so a themed area inside another (like the style page previews) gets its own colors.
