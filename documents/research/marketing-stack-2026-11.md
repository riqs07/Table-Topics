# Marketing Stack: What Is Worth Paying For (November 2026)

**Purpose:** Which paid services give the most for a small budget at each step of the funnel (seen, followed, website, newsletter, paying user), given what VoiceLab already pays for. Written after the first two social reels (decision 157).
**Status:** Research, not a decision. Prices are from third-party trackers and vendor pages read on 2026-11-04; most were not confirmed on the vendor's own pricing page, so check there before paying.
**Already paid for:** Claude Max ($100 a month), website hosting, ElevenLabs (plan not recorded here).

## Summary

Spend on the voice and nothing else yet. The reels are made in Remotion (free for a company of up to three people) by Claude, so the only production cost per reel is the voice: about 5 to 8 cents a take. AI video generators are now cheap per second, but their real cost is retries and a character that changes between clips, so they are worth buying per clip for backgrounds and openers only, not as a subscription. Scheduling, the newsletter and TikTok's music are free at this size. Paid promotion is the one place money would widen the funnel, and it should wait until organic posts show which reel holds attention.

## What each step needs, and the best value found

| Step | Need | Best value | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Make the reel | Animation, captions, charts | Remotion + Claude (what we use now) | $0 extra | Remotion is free for companies of up to three people; a larger company needs its licence |
| Make the reel | Voice | ElevenLabs **Starter** | about $6 a month | The cheapest tier with a commercial licence; 30,000 credits a month. A 16-second reel took about 300 credits a take, so roughly 100 takes a month. **The free plan has no commercial licence**, so ads voiced on it are not licensed; the licence covers audio made while the paid plan is active |
| Make the reel | AI footage (backgrounds, an opener) | Pay per clip through one API (fal.ai hosts Hailuo, Wan, Kling, Seedance and Veo behind one key) | about $0.02 to $0.09 a second | Cheapest found: Hailuo 02 at 512p about $0.017 a second, Wan 2.2 Turbo about $0.026 a second, Veo 3.1 Lite about $0.03 a second (720p, no sound). A 5-second draft costs about 10 to 15 cents. Use a cheap tier for drafts and pay for 720p on the final clip only |
| Make the reel | AI footage, as a subscription | Google AI Pro | $19.99 a month | Reported to include about 50 Veo 3 Fast videos a month (unconfirmed; one source reports a daily cap). Only worth it if Gemini is also useful to you |
| Make the reel | Music | TikTok Commercial Music Library | free | Cleared for organic posts and paid ads **on TikTok only**; not for the same video on Instagram or YouTube |
| Make the reel | Music, any platform | Suno **Pro** | about $10 a month | Commercial rights for songs made while subscribed; one month could make a library of beds. One source reports a new cap of 20 downloads a month on Pro (unconfirmed) |
| Post it | Scheduling | Buffer free plan | $0 | 3 channels (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and others), 10 queued posts per channel. Metricool's free plan is an alternative; sources disagree on its limits |
| Grow it | Paid promotion | Boost only a reel that already holds attention organically | your choice | TikTok Ads Manager campaigns are reported to need about $50 a day at campaign level ($20 per ad group), too much for a test. Spark Ads promote an existing post and keep its likes. Meta's boost minimums were not found in this search |
| Capture | Newsletter | beehiiv free plan | $0 up to about 2,500 subscribers | Kit's free plan is reported at up to 10,000 subscribers (one source, unconfirmed). Either is free until the list is large |

## Not worth it yet

- **Subscription video studios** (Higgsfield and similar): they bundle many models for a monthly fee, but the reels don't need generated footage to work, and pay-per-clip is cheaper at a few clips a month.
- **ElevenLabs Creator ($22) or above:** Starter's credits cover about 100 reel takes a month. Creator adds professional voice cloning, which the reels don't use.
- **Making whole reels from AI video:** a 60-second reel is about 12 clips; at about 4 tries each, that is about 50 generations a reel, and the blob would not stay the same from clip to clip (decision 143).

## A suggested monthly budget

| Item | Monthly |
|---|---|
| ElevenLabs Starter (if not already on a paid plan) | about $6 |
| AI footage experiments, capped | up to $10 |
| Suno Pro, for one month only, to make a music library | $10 once |
| Scheduling, newsletter, TikTok music | $0 |
| **New spending** | **about $6 to $16, plus $10 once** |

Paid promotion comes on top, once a reel has proved itself organically. That is the step that most directly widens the top of the funnel.
